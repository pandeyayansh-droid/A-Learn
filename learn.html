Chapter 1 — Geography

India is located in South Asia. Its major physical divisions include the Himalayas, Northern Plains, Peninsular Plateau, Indian Desert, Coastal Plains and Islands.


Chapter 2 — History

European trading companies came to India for trade. The English East India Company gradually increased its political and economic control.
